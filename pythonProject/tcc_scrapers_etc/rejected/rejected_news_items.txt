Fried Rice recipe:

1.cut the vegetables: bell peppers - long cuts and then bunch them and short transverse cuts (total about 1/2 of one bell pepper)
baby carrots - 4-5 --> cut each into 4 small pieces
broccoli: - 4-5 florets - cut or snap into pieces of your choice

After cutting, put them in a ceramic bowl, sprinkle water and microwave for 3-3.5 min.
After this, take your pre-cooked rice in a bowl and sprinkle water lightly and microwave for 1 min and keep it aside.

2. Take 1 cm of butter cut and put it into the pan - high heat - once it melts, add a sprinkle of cumin seeds. Add mushrooms - 4-5 mushrooms, snap them into smaller pieces
Add salt - you have to figure out how many times you sprinkle - depending on the amount that comes out with each sprinkle - i normally sprinkle (shaking the bottle) 4-5 times
At this stage, you can add the sliced chicken pieces. After mushroom, chicken and salt, stir for 2-3 min

3. Once the mushroom stirring is don, add the boiled veg pieces. stir it for 2 min. 

4. Add the red masala --> sprinkle with two of the larger holes exposed (from the glass bottle in which it is kept - it has three different sizes of holes - use 2 of the larger holes open and sprinkle 2-3 times.

5. stir this mixture for 1-2 min, such that the red spice is uniformly spread to all the vegetables. 

6. then add the pre-heated rice and then stir for 1-2 min (if you see the mixture getting dry then add 1/2 cm of butter and let it melt while stirring. 

Your rice is ready
